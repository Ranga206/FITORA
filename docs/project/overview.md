# Project Overview: Fitora

### Tagline: Eat Better • Move Better • Live Better (PROPOSED — NOT APPROVED)

---

## 🌟 Executive Summary

**Fitora** is a holistic, unified wellness and lifestyle platform designed to make healthy living approachable, sustainable, and rewarding. Most existing wellness apps exist in extreme silos—either strictly counting calories, tracking gym reps, or managing generic habit checkboxes.

Fitora unifies health into **Three Core Pillars**:
1. **Nutrition**: Guiding mindful food choices, calorie understanding, and meal logging.
2. **Fitness**: Offering an exercise library, structured workout plans, and session tracking.
3. **Awareness**: Imparting bite-sized, evidence-based lessons so users understand *why* healthy habits matter, not just *what* tasks to perform.

These three pillars are fortified by three **Supporting Systems**:
- **Habit Tracking**: Anchoring daily lifestyle routines.
- **Progress Tracking**: Aggregating trends across diet, exercise, and habits.
- **Gamification**: Rewarding sustainable consistency through XP, levels, streaks, achievements, challenges, and competitive leagues.

---

## 🎯 Target Audience

- **Everyday Wellness Seekers**: Individuals who want a balanced lifestyle without feeling intimidated by hardcore bodybuilding tools or extreme dieting calculators.
- **Inconsistent Habit Builders**: Users who start wellness routines but drop off because of fragmented tools and lack of continuous engagement.
- **Learners Seeking Guidance**: People who want to understand the rationale behind macronutrients, resistance training, sleep hygiene, and hydration.

---

## 💡 Core Philosophy

1. **Holistic Balance Over Extremism**: Health is not just working out or just dieting. Fitora discourages crash diets and extreme overtraining.
2. **Action Driven by Awareness**: Long-term behavior change requires cognitive understanding. When users learn *why* protein aids recovery or *why* sleep regulates metabolism, consistency improves.
3. **Rewarding Consistency, Not Genetics**: Gamification (XP, Leagues) is calibrated on actions completed (logging, workouts, habit streaks), not body weight or aesthetic metrics.

---

## 🧩 Major Application Modules

1. **Dashboard**: Central command center showing daily goals, streak status, active league rank, and quick actions.
2. **Nutrition Hub**: Meal planner, calorie/macro breakdown, food log, and nutritional insights.
3. **Fitness Hub**: Exercise library, workout generator/planner, and active workout logger.
4. **Awareness Center**: Curated library of bite-sized wellness articles and educational lessons.
5. **Habit Center**: Daily lifestyle habit checklist with frequency scheduling and streak monitoring.
6. **Progress Analytics**: Interactive visualization of multi-week trends (calories, workout volume, habit completion).
7. **Gamification & Leagues**: XP progression, level milestones, unlocked achievements, active challenges, and league standings.
8. **Profile & Settings**: Biometric context (height, weight, age, activity level), goal preferences, and account management.

---

## 🔮 Future Scope Notice

- **Artificial Intelligence (AI)**: Personalised AI meal recommendations and dynamic AI workout coaches are strictly designated as **future optional extensions**. Initial implementation is built on deterministic calculations and structured libraries.
