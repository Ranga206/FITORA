# Core Pillar 3: Awareness

This document outlines the functional and educational specifications for the **Awareness** pillar of **Fitora**.

---

## 🧠 Overview & Purpose: Understanding the "WHY"

Awareness is the third core pillar of Fitora and a key differentiator of the platform.

Most health tools tell users **WHAT** to do (*"drink 3 liters of water," "eat 150g protein," "do not eat past 8 PM"*). However, when habits feel arbitrary, users quickly abandon them. Fitora focuses on educating users on the **WHY**:
- *Why* does sleep directly regulate hunger hormones (leptin and ghrelin)?
- *Why* does resistance training preserve bone mineral density as we age?
- *Why* are whole foods more satiating than ultra-processed alternatives?

When users understand the biological and cognitive mechanisms behind healthy choices, intrinsic motivation replaces fleeting willpower.

### 1. Nutrition Awareness
- Explaining caloric balance, thermodynamics, and realistic deficit rates.
- The biological roles of protein, dietary fats, and complex carbohydrates.
- The thermic effect of food (TEF) and the satiety index.
- Debunking common dietary myths (e.g., carbs make you fat, detox cleanses, spot reduction).

### 2. Fitness Awareness
- The principle of progressive overload and mechanical tension.
- Rest and recovery: Why muscles grow during sleep and recovery, not during the workout.
- Aerobic endurance vs. anaerobic training and cardiovascular health.
- Mobility, joint health, and injury prevention principles.

### 3. Lifestyle Awareness
- Sleep architecture: Deep sleep, REM cycles, and circadian rhythm alignment.
- Hydration and electrolyte balance: Cognitive impact of mild dehydration.
- Stress management: The effects of chronic cortisol on fat distribution and recovery.
- Behavioral psychology: How habit loops (cue, routine, reward) dictate daily choices.

## 📖 Lesson Structure & User Experience

To avoid cognitive fatigue, Awareness content follows strict design guidelines:
- **Format**: Bite-sized, 2 to 3-minute reading time per lesson.
- **Tone**: Engaging, accessible, evidence-informed, and practical. No dense medical jargon.
- **Key Takeaways**: Every lesson concludes with 3 actionable bullet points.
- **Completion Tracking**: Users can mark articles as "Read & Understood".
